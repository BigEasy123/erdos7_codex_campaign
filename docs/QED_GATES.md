# QED gates

**No agent may write “QED” unless all gates below are PROVED_EXACT or PROVED_SYMBOLIC and independently audited.**

## Gate 1 — state-2275 correlated arbitrary-depth closure

For every legal arbitrary-depth continuation of state 2275, prove that it cannot remain both covering-capable and on every required analytic bad side. A valid closure may be a finite exhaustive certificate only if it is shown to dominate every legal arbitrary-depth continuation.

## Gate 2 — lift from state 2275 to all 7,637 canonical low-four partials

Prove a theorem that the certified local obstruction(s) cover all canonical states. “State 2275 has the smallest tower reserve” is not automatically a concentration-domination theorem.

## Gate 3 — finite-prime analytic interface

Audit the stagewise low-prefix/HN/BBMST mechanism through primes 13–73 with exact arithmetic or exact certificates where needed. Verify repeated powers are treated by a theorem that actually allows them.

## Gate 4 — >73 termination

Identify the precise published theorem(s), hypotheses, and mapping from the project state to those hypotheses. No informal “BBMST takes over” or “HN takes over”.

## Gate 5 — structural reductions

Line-by-line audit of least counterexample, irredundancy, prime support, compression, hub/blocker, chain/fork, pivot, and first-exhaustion reductions used in the final proof.

## Gate 6 — independent replay / referee

A clean checkout must reproduce all claimed exact finite results without trusting optimizer status codes. A separate referee track must attempt to falsify every nontrivial interface and certify dependency closure.

Only after Gates 1–6 are closed may the project status be `QED_CANDIDATE`. Publication-level “QED” requires a final human mathematical audit as well.
